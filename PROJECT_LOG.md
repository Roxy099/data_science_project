\### Key Learnings

\- A machine learning project begins with understanding the business problem, not writing code.

\- The objective is to reduce financial losses while continuing to approve creditworthy customers.

\- Machine learning supports decision-making rather than replacing it.

\- Different stakeholders (loan officers, risk managers, senior management, customers, etc.) use the model in different ways.

\- ROI is an important business metric used to evaluate whether a project delivers value.



\### Decisions Made

\- The project will follow an enterprise workflow rather than a Kaggle-only workflow.

\- CSV files will be used initially for data understanding and model development.

\- MySQL integration will be introduced later to simulate an industry workflow.

\- The project duration is planned for 45 days.

\- Every technical decision will be documented with its business rationale.
